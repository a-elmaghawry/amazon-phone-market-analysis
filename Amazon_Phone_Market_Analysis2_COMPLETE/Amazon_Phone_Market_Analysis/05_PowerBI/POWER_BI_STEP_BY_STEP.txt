## 4) Measures
انسخ Measures التالية واحدة واحدة داخل Modeling > New measure:

```DAX
TOTAL PRODUCTS =
DISTINCTCOUNT(PhoneProducts[asin])

AVERAGE PRICE =
AVERAGE(PhoneProducts[product_price_num])

AVERAGE RATING =
AVERAGE(PhoneProducts[product_star_rating])

TOTAL RATINGS =
SUM(PhoneProducts[product_num_ratings])

AVERAGE OFFERS =
AVERAGE(PhoneProducts[product_num_offers])

PRIME PRODUCTS =
CALCULATE([TOTAL PRODUCTS], PhoneProducts[is_prime] = TRUE())

BEST SELLERS =
CALCULATE([TOTAL PRODUCTS], PhoneProducts[is_best_seller] = TRUE())

DISCOUNTED PRODUCTS =
CALCULATE([TOTAL PRODUCTS], PhoneProducts[has_discount] = TRUE())

DISCOUNT % =
DIVIDE([DISCOUNTED PRODUCTS], [TOTAL PRODUCTS])

```
